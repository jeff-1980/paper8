# Archived results (not part of the peer-reviewed paper)

`archive_supplement.pdf` contains results that do not bear on the sensor-configuration question of the
MST manuscript and were not submitted for peer review: comparisons of the learners with one another and
with normalisation variants, a forward-pass latency benchmark, and an exploratory kinematic
frequency-alignment regulariser for the Mamba-3 learner. Under the leakage-free protocol the regulariser
gave no consistent gain in accuracy or inter-seed variance and is not a contribution of this work.
